The 8472: Where would this make a difference?

The 8472: this code:

```rust

```

```
[src/main.rs:3:5] (-0.0_f32).min(0.0).max(0.0) = 0.0
[src/main.rs:4:5] (-0.0_f32).minimum(0.0).maximum(0.0) = 0.0
[src/main.rs:5:5] (-0.0_f32).clamp(0.0, 0.0) = -0.0
```
