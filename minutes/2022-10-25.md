### ACPs

Rename AtomicPtr::fetch_ptr_{add,sub} to AtomicPtr::fetch_{add,sub}: https://github.com/rust-lang/libs-team/issues/126

On regular pointers we have `add` and `byte_add`, but for AtomicPtr we have `fetch_ptr_add` and `fetch_byte_add`. `fetch_add` might be confusing, but would be more consistent.

It's quite common to use AtomicPtr for something that contains both a pointer and flags.
